Common questions

{{#LOGIN_REQUIRED}}

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and signs you out everywhere. You can also ask us by email.

How do I get a copy of my data?

In the app, open Settings and tap Export my data. You get a file with what we hold about your account.

{{/LOGIN_REQUIRED}}{{^LOGIN_REQUIRED}}

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no accounts: your shelf, hums and songs are kept on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

{{/LOGIN_REQUIRED}}

The app can't hear me, or says the microphone is off

On iPhone, open the Settings app, choose {{APP_NAME}}, and switch Microphone on. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Microphone, and switch {{APP_NAME}} on. Also check that no other app, call or recording is using the microphone, then try again.

A hum{{#TALK_MODE}} or a spoken reply{{/TALK_MODE}} "isn't on the server any more"

For your privacy, hum recordings and the files made from them are deleted automatically about {{HUM_RETENTION}} after they were last used. Hum again to start a new one.

The app can't reach the server

Check your internet connection and try again in a minute. The server may be restarting.

A score is wrong, or shouldn't be in the library

Email us the title and composer and what is wrong. We review every report.
