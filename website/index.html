What you can do

  • Hum mode: hum a melody and the app turns it into a song you can play along with.
  • Practice feedback: play your instrument into the microphone and see what you got right.
  • Library and shelf: browse public-domain scores and keep your favourites.
  • Quiz: generate music-history questions from a topic.

Hum mode, the library and the quiz need an internet connection, because they use the Hu Accomponist server.

Install

  1. Download the .dmg above, open it, and drag the app onto Applications.
  2. The first time you open it, macOS will refuse, because the app is not signed with an Apple Developer ID. To allow it, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about the app and click Open Anyway. Or in Terminal run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/hu_accomponist.app and open it again.
  3. Allow the microphone when asked. Hum mode and practice need it.

Only install apps you trust. This one is open source: its code and the build that made your download are public on GitHub. To check your download was not damaged, save the checksum file next to the .dmg and run shasum -a 256 -c <name>.dmg.sha256 in Terminal.

Your recordings

When you play an instrument, the app analyses the sound on your computer and sends only the notes it detected. When you hum, the recording goes to our server to be turned into a song, and is deleted automatically after a short while. The Privacy Policy has the details.
