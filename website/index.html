How it reads you

Every note gets an answer the moment you play it. Nothing covers the music but the words.

  1. Right, on time

    The whole note fills green.

  2. A little off

    The right key, a touch early or late: yellow.

  3. Early, late, missed

    The note stays as written; a small word under it says what happened.

  4. Wrong key

    It names the key you pressed and the one written.

  5. Repeats

    The bars go back to black and fill in again. Your summary keeps every time through.

  6. What’s next

    The notes to play now keep jumping until you play them.
