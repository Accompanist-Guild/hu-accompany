Library and shelf

Find sheet music, keep favourites, and keep your hummed songs.

Search the library

  1. Open Search (the page is titled MUSIC LIBRARY).
  2. Type a title or a composer, for example chopin or ballade. Every word you type has to match the title or composer.
  3. Results come best-rated first. Tap one to open it, then play along from Practice.
  4. Tap the star on a result to make it a favourite.

Before you search, the page shows Featured scores, or Recently played once you have played some, with your favourites in their own list. If nothing matches you will see No sheet music found. Try fewer or different words.

Where the music comes from

The library is a selection of more than a thousand scores from PDMX, a public-domain MusicXML collection (Long et al., 2024, licensed CC BY 4.0). We kept only a small part of it.

The collection's public-domain labels are set by the people who uploaded the scores, so we screened them ourselves. We offer only scores that are not flagged for a licence conflict and whose composer lived long enough ago for the music to be in the public domain. That is why you will find Chopin, Mozart, Bach and Debussy, but not modern songs.

Spotted a mistake? A screen like this can still make errors, and a modern typeset edition may carry its own rights. If a score is wrong or should not be here, use Settings › Contact support or send feedback and we will look at it promptly.

Your shelf

The Shelf keeps what you want to come back to. It has two tabs:

  • Sheets: scores you opened from the library. If it is empty, it says Your shelf is empty: open a score from the library and it will appear here.
  • Hummed: songs you saved from Hum. Tap one to play it.

Your shelf, your hummed songs and the notes you draw on scores are stored on your Mac only. Deleting the app's data removes them.
