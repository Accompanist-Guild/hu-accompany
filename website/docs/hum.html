Hum a song

Hum a tune and get a song to play along with.

Make a song

  1. Open Hum.
  2. Hold the button where it says Hold to hum, and hum your tune. It says Listening... let go when you are done.
  3. Let go. The app says Finding your tune... and then Making your song.... This takes a few seconds, and a little longer if the server has been idle.
  4. Your song appears under YOUR SONG. Play it.

The app stops listening after 15 seconds, so hum a short phrase. Anything shorter than a fifth of a second is too short to hold a note.

Hum so it works

  • Use a quiet room, and keep the same distance from the microphone.
  • Hum steady notes on one syllable such as "la" or "doo", with a small gap between notes.
  • One melody at a time. The app listens for a single tune, not chords.
  • Not happy with the result? Hum again to try a new one.

Three song makers

The app can turn the same hum into a song in three ways. Switch between them above your song, and use Compare to see what each one does.

BandA band plays your tune in a genre, with an intro, verse, chorus and outro. Each instrument is its own part, so you can change one at a time.
EpicA full orchestra arranged around your tune. The biggest sound, and best from a clean hum.
SimpleChords in a style under your tune. The steadiest choice for a rough hum.

Shape the song

  • Band: pick a genre and mood. You can also change one instrument, its volume, the tempo, the key or a section by chat (see Talk mode).
  • Epic and Simple: use the sliders for Mood (toward Bright), Speed (Slower or Faster) and Pitch (toward Higher).
  • Undo and Redo step through your changes. Start over (Band) or Reset returns to the song as it first came out.

Good to know. Epic and Simple make the song in one piece, so edits you made in Band are not in them. They are kept: switch back to Band to hear them again.

Your hum, as hummed

Below the song, YOUR HUM, AS HUMMED shows the notes the app found. Choose Play my hum to hear them back, or Export MIDI to take the melody into other music software.

Keep a song

Save a song and it appears on your Shelf, under Hummed, with the app's confirmation Saved to your shelf, under Hummed.

What happens to your recording

When you hum, the recording is sent to our server over an encrypted connection to be turned into notes and a song. It is deleted automatically after about three hours. If you come back to a hum later and the app says it isn't on the server any more, just hum again. The Privacy Policy link in the footer has the details.
