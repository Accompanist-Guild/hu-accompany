Talk mode

Change a song by typing or speaking. For people 18 and over.

Talk mode lets you reshape a song you hummed in plain words, for example softer drums, make it jazz. You type a request, or speak it, and the song changes.

Who it is for

Talk mode is for people 18 or older. It uses two outside AI services, Google Gemini and ElevenLabs, and both require that their users are adults. The rest of the app does not need Talk mode and is not limited this way.

The first time you use it

The app shows a box titled Share your requests with AI services?. It explains what Talk mode sends:

  • Your request, and a description of your song (such as its style, instruments and tempo), go to Google Gemini, which works out what to change.
  • If you speak, your voice recording goes to ElevenLabs, which turns it into text and speaks the replies.
  • Your hum recordings are not sent to either of them.

The box also says that both companies handle this data under their own terms, and that ElevenLabs may keep your voice recording and use it to improve its own services. Read it before you agree.

Nothing is sent, and the microphone does not start for Talk mode, until you tick I am 18 or older and choose Agree and continue. Choose Not now and nothing is sent. You can read the Privacy Policy from the box without answering.

Using it

  1. Hum a song on the Hum page.
  2. Below your song, find CHANGE IT WITH WORDS. Type a request, or use the microphone button to speak one.
  3. The song changes and the app replies, in text and in a spoken voice.

Chat edits work on the Band song maker, because Band keeps each instrument as its own part. If you ask for something that needs Band, the app switches to it and tells you. Every change can be undone.

The AI can get things wrong or misunderstand you. Please do not put sensitive personal information into your requests.

Turning it off

Open Settings, find Talk mode and AI services under Account & data, and choose Stop sharing. Talk mode stops working until you agree again, and the app asks you the next time you use it. This stops anything more being sent. It cannot take back what was already sent.
