Getting started

Install the Mac app and find your way around.

What you need

  • A Mac running macOS 12 (Monterey) or later. Apple silicon and Intel Macs both work.
  • An internet connection. The songs, practice feedback, library and quiz all come from the Hu Accomponist server.
  • A microphone. The one built into your Mac is fine. The app uses the input your Mac is set to use.

Install

  1. On the home page, choose Download for Mac. You get a .dmg file.
  2. Open the .dmg and drag the app onto Applications.
  3. Optional: check the download was not damaged. Save the checksum file next to the .dmg and, in Terminal, run shasum -a 256 -c <name>.dmg.sha256. It should say OK.

Open it the first time

macOS will refuse to open the app the first time, because it is not signed with an Apple Developer ID. That is expected for this app. To allow it:

  1. Try to open the app once, then close the warning.
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security (on macOS 12, System Preferences › Security & Privacy) and scroll down to the message about the app.
  3. Click Open Anyway and confirm.

Or, in Terminal, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/hu_accomponist.app and open the app again. You only need to do this once.

Only install apps you trust. This one is open source, so you can read its code and the build that made your download on GitHub.

Allow the microphone

Hum and Practice need the microphone. Choose Allow when macOS asks. If you chose Don't Allow by mistake, see the microphone fix.

Take the tutorial

The first time you open the app, a short tutorial shows you how to move around. You can skip it, and you can replay it any time in Settings › Replay the tutorial.

Finding your way around

The app looks like a turntable. A row of records along the bottom takes you between pages, and a drawer called All pages lists them too.

HomePick up where you left off.
PracticePlay along with a score or an exercise. Guide
SearchFind sheet music in the library. Guide
ShelfYour saved scores and hummed songs. Guide
QuizTest your theory and history. Guide
HumHum a tune and get a song. Guide
SettingsTutorial, legal pages, Talk mode permission, support.

Updating and removing

  • Update: download the new version from the home page and drag it onto Applications, replacing the old one. Your shelf and settings stay in place.
  • Remove: drag the app to the Bin. Your shelf and settings are kept in the app's data folder under ~/Library/Containers until you delete that too.
