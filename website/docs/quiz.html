Quiz

Practice music theory and history with questions built from a topic.

Start a quiz

  1. Open Quiz (MUSIC THEORY & HISTORY).
  2. In Topic, type what you want to study, or tap one of the examples under Try one of these.
  3. Under Questions, choose how many questions you want.
  4. Choose Begin quiz.

Topics

The quiz covers music history by era. These examples are built in:

  • The Middle Ages (AD 476 – AD 1450)
  • The Renaissance
  • The Baroque Era
  • The Classical Era
  • The Romantic Era
  • The Twentieth Century

If you are studying for an exam, you can put a heading line above the era, the way the example does: RCM HISTORY 10 + ARCT CHEAT SHEET. If the app does not recognise the era, it tells you which eras it does cover.

After the quiz

The results page shows how you did by category, then a Review of the questions you missed or skipped, with what you answered. If you got everything right it says Every question correct. Nothing to review.

Quizzes are made on the server from a question bank, so they need an internet connection. Your topic is used to make the quiz and is not linked to you or kept as a history.
